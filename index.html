Our aim

The Advent message to all the world in my generation.

Our motto

The love of Christ compels us.

The pledge

By the grace of God, I will be pure and kind and true. I will keep the Pathfinder Law. I will be a servant of God and a friend to man.

What Pathfinders do

With nearly 2 million members worldwide, the club welcomes any young person who promises to follow the Pathfinder Pledge and Law, whatever their church background.

  • Outdoor adventureCamping, hiking, canoeing, and backpacking trips with your club.
  • Learning and skillsEarn honors and class awards in crafts, nature, health, and more.
  • Community serviceSpot a need in your neighborhood and take action.
  • Faith and friendshipGrow closer to God and to people who share your values.
